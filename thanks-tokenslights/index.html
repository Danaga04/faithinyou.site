Frequently asked questions

When will my Light Tokens arrive?
Within about 1 minute of your payment being confirmed. If you don't see them, close the app completely and open it again — they appear automatically. No code to enter.
I don't see my tokens yet — what do I do?
Three quick things: (1) wait up to a minute, (2) close and reopen the app, and (3) make sure you're logged in with the exact email you purchased with. A different email opens an empty account. Still missing after that? Contact us below with your purchase email and we'll sort it out.
Which email should I log in with?
Always the same email you used at checkout. Your tokens are tied to that email, so logging in with another one won't show them.
What are Light Tokens (✦)?
They're the light you use inside the app — to open a session, light a candle, send a blessing, or talk with Mark. Your tokens stay in your account and never expire. Whatever you unlock with them is yours to keep.
What will I see on my bank statement?
Your payment is handled by Digistore24, so the charge appears as Digistore24 (it may read "DIGISTORE24" or "DS24 Inc") — not "Sacred Chant". Your emailed receipt will say Sacred Chant. If a charge looks unfamiliar, it's us — just reach out below.
How do I keep the app handy on my phone?
Open the app, then look for "How to install the app on your phone" in the Support & blessings section — it shows you how to add Sacred Chant to your home screen, one tap away.
