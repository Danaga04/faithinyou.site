Why do we use Light Tokens? ✦

Light Tokens exist to protect your journey — and your results.

When people rush through every prayer and video all at once, they consume everything and get no real results. A little at a time, with intention, is what brings true change. Quality matters more than quantity.

Tokens also protect the app from bad actors who would otherwise sign in only to download all the content at once and spread it across the internet — taking these sacred prayers away from the people they were made for.

So each token is a gentle pace-keeper: it helps you go deeper, one step at a time, and keeps Sacred Chant safe for everyone.
