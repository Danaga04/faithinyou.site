The Sealing Prayer

Saint Michael, at the door of my house

Press play, put your hand over your heart and say it with Celestino. One minute, somewhere quiet.

Saint Michael, Archangel of the Lord,
you who cast the serpent down —
stand now at the door of my house.
Seal these walls. Seal those I love.
What was handed down to me in sorrow,
let it end here, in my hands.
I am not what I carried.
I am sealed. My house is sealed.
In the name of the Father, and of the Son,
and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

📖 Three-minute morning practice
  1. Arrive. Sit with feet on the floor, shoulders soft, breathe naturally.
  2. Attend. Feel your feet, rest on one quiet point, return to the next breath.
  3. Intend. Name what you want to bring today: patience, gratitude, care. Say a short prayer, then choose a chant.
🕊️ Natural elements handbook

Choose a crystal or smooth stone with meaning for you and place it beside your prayer materials. Set a plant nearby as a reminder of patience. Keep water away from electronics. Pick one intention, say it in a short prayer, listen to a chant, then return to your intention. Incense is optional.
